BeBold settings

BeBold bolds the start of each word to give your eye an anchor. Some people find it easier to hold their place. The published research has not found a reading-speed benefit, so treat it as a preference rather than a technique, and keep it only if it helps you.

Appearance

Changes preview live below and apply to open tabs immediately.

Reading long passages of text on a screen is hard when your attention keeps sliding off the line. A small anchor at the start of each word can give your eye somewhere to land.
Writing systems

These are off by default because "the first half of a word" is not a meaningful visual unit in them, not because they are unsupported. Turn one on if it works for you.

Sites

BeBold always stays off where a page asks for a password or card number, and where a site opts out. Those are automatic and are not listed here.

Privacy

BeBold makes no network requests of any kind. It collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores only the preferences on this page in Chrome's own sync storage.
